Attack analysis — why this works
1
Fake domain: "micros0ft-accounts.com" uses a zero instead of "o". Most people never check the full sender address.
2
Urgency trigger: "Account locked in 24 hours" forces a rushed decision. Panic overrides rational thinking — the core of social engineering.
3
Malicious link: URL looks official but "account-alert.net" is attacker-controlled. Leads to a fake login page that harvests credentials.
4
Personalization: Uses real name and company email. Attackers scrape LinkedIn to make emails feel personal and targeted.
5
Professional branding: Real Microsoft colors, logo, and legal footer. Visual design disarms suspicion before the victim reads the content.
